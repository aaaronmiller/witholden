The problem

1099 income makes you a business

Withholden gives your sole-proprietor work a bookkeeping and tax-prep system, so tax season stops living in your head.

You are the business

The IRS generally treats 1099 self-employment income as sole-proprietor business income, with Schedule C records to keep.

No payroll department

A W-2 job withholds taxes for you. Gig payouts land whole, so bookkeeping and estimated-tax planning fall on you.

Tax season becomes work

Without ongoing books, deductions, and payment planning, filing season turns into a scramble through old payouts and receipts.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is this a bank account?

Withholden is not a bank. The tax savings account is powered by Stripe Treasury; Withholden partners with Stripe Payments Company for money transmission services and account services with funds held at Fifth Third Bank, N.A., Member FDIC.

How does Withholden know which deposits are taxable?

You tag your 1099 business income the first time it appears. After that, Withholden recognizes recurring gig sources so your sole-proprietor books and estimated-tax plan stay current.

How does Withholden use AI?

Withholden is AI native — AI is built into how we do bookkeeping, recognize gig income, and keep deduction context current, not bolted on. It never acts alone: money moves only at your authorized percentage, tax guidance is reviewed under registered and bonded California tax preparers, and you approve anything that gets filed or paid.

What percentage is charged?

You choose the tax savings percentage. We suggest a planning rate from current IRS tables for self-employment income, and you can adjust it as your business earnings change.

Is connecting my bank safe?

Yes. Connections use bank-level encryption and are powered by Stripe, the same payments infrastructure trusted by millions of businesses. We never store your bank login.

Do I still have to file my taxes?

Yes. Withholden provides bookkeeping, planning, filing-support, and payment-support workflows so taxes stop being a surprise, but you are still responsible for reviewing, authorizing, filing, and paying correctly.

What does it cost?

Both subscriptions start with 30 days free. Withholden Pro is $8.99 per month, and Hands Free Taxes is $15.99 per month for full-service tax preparation and estimated-payment support. No per-payout fees. Cancel any time.
