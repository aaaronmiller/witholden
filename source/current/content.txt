THE PROBLEM

1099 income makes you a business

Withholden gives your sole-proprietor work a bookkeeping and tax-prep system, so tax season stops living in your head.

You are the business

The IRS generally treats 1099 self-employment income as sole-proprietor business income, with Schedule C records to keep.

No payroll department

A W-2 job withholds taxes for you. Gig payouts land whole, so bookkeeping and estimated-tax planning fall on you.

Tax season becomes work

Without ongoing books, deductions, and payment planning, filing season turns into a scramble through old payouts and receipts.
